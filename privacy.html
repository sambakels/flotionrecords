3. Legal basis for processing

  • Contract performance (Art. 6.1.b GDPR): for paid Track Review submissions, curator agreements, custom music orders, and sync licensing.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f GDPR): for free demo submissions, curator applications, and basic security logs. We balance our interest in running a label against your privacy.
  • Consent (Art. 6.1.a GDPR): for Google Analytics and any optional cookies. You can withdraw consent at any time via the cookie banner or by clearing your browser data.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6.1.c GDPR): for tax and accounting records (we must retain invoices for 7 years under Dutch law).

7. Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: correct any inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure ("right to be forgotten"): request deletion, subject to legal retention obligations.
  • Restriction: limit how we use your data.
  • Data portability: receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection: object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Withdraw consent: for any consent-based processing, at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email contact@flotionrecords.com. We respond within 30 days. There is no charge for reasonable requests.
