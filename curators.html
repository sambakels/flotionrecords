FAQ

Common questions

When and how do I get paid?
Once your earnings reach €25, we pay out via SEPA bank transfer in the first week of the next month. Payouts are free within the EU. We'll move to automated payouts via Stripe Connect once volume justifies it.
What if a submission isn't a fit for my playlist?
Reject it with a short honest note (1 sentence is fine). You still earn your share for the review — you got paid for your listening time, not for adding the track. That's the whole point of paid review.
Can I decline submissions I don't have time for?
Yes — if you can't review within the SLA, hit "Pass to next curator" within 6 hours of receiving it. The submission gets re-routed and you don't take a strike. Don't ghost submissions: missing the SLA = automatic refund to the artist + a strike against you.
Why 60/40 and not 50/50 like SubmitHub or Groover?
Because we want to attract serious curators with quality playlists. Higher curator share = better incentive to do quality reviews. The 40% on Flotion's side covers Stripe fees (~3%), the SLA refund pool, marketing to bring in artists, and platform development.
Are there any fees to apply or stay listed?
No. Application is free. There are no monthly fees. You only earn money — you never pay us anything.
What about Spotify's "no paid placement" policy?
Artists pay for our (and your) listening time + written feedback. Whether the track ends up on your playlist is your independent curatorial decision — not something the payment guarantees. This is the same model SubmitHub, Groover and Daily Playlists operate under, and it's compliant with Spotify's terms when run honestly.
